THE LOOK — THIS IS WHY EVERY PROMPT SOUNDS SIMILAR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The whole site runs on ONE idea, taken from your reference photo:

    a cool, sharp screen sitting inside a warm, soft room

Warm = honey amber, oak wood, cream, low tungsten light, out-of-focus depth.
Cool = near-black screens with blue/green/cyan syntax colour.

Palette to keep saying in prompts:
    honey amber   #E5A445     (the lamp — this is the brand colour)
    oak           #C6A079
    cream         #F3EBDF
    espresso      #1D1814
    screen black  #13161B
    cool daylight #CFDDE4

ALWAYS end every prompt with the NEGATIVE line. AI video/image models love
adding garbled fake text and logos, and that will ruin the shot.

IF A RESULT COMES OUT WRONG
===============================================================================

Garbled fake text appeared
  -> add to NEGATIVE: no text, no letters, no symbols, no signage, no captions

Too bright / washed out
  -> add: low key lighting, deep shadows, underexposed, moody

Too orange, looks like a filter
  -> replace "honey amber" with "warm tungsten" and add: natural colour grade,
     not oversaturated

Camera moves too fast
  -> add: extremely slow camera movement, almost still, locked-off tripod feel

Faces appear when you did not want them
  -> add: no people, no faces, no humans, empty room

Looks like generic stock footage
  -> add: shot on 35mm film, subtle grain, imperfect framing, candid
