# Frequently Asked Questions

All your questions answered. Still confused? Reach out to us on WhatsApp or via our website.

#### What is included in this internship + course program?

You'll go through structured training: 30 days of live and recorded classes with assignments and interactive sessions, followed by 30+ days of real-time project sprints with daily tasks and meetings.

#### What technologies will I learn?

You'll master GitHub, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Python, FastAPI, SQL, MongoDB (via ORMs), Docker, and Kubernetes — preparing you to build full-stack apps confidently.

#### What do I receive after completion?

You'll get a Course Completion Certificate, Internship Certificate, and a Letter of Recommendation. We also help with resume building and LinkedIn profile optimization.

#### How is the training conducted?

We use a blended model: Live classes for 30 days (also recorded), followed by daily sprint-based project training. This structure helps you apply what you learn, just like in real jobs.

#### Is this a paid program?

Yes, the total program fee is just ₹2000 — which includes the full course, internship training, certificates, mentorship, and career support.

#### Will I get placement support?

Yes, we provide career guidance through resume prep, LinkedIn profile building, mock interviews, and referrals for opportunities through our growing partner network.

#### Do I need prior experience to join?

No prior experience is required. Whether you're from a CS background or not, we train you from basics to advanced with full support at every step.

#### How do I enroll?

You can enroll directly via our website at https://zeazh.com/internship/PaymentForm or contact us through WhatsApp for help.

# Full Stack Developer Intern

Join our team as a Full Stack Developer Intern and contribute to building innovative solutions.

## About the Internship

We are looking for a motivated and driven Full Stack Developer Intern to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will have the opportunity to work on real-world projects and contribute to building full-stack web applications using modern technologies.

## Key Responsibilities

- Collaborate with front-end and back-end developers to create full-stack applications.

- Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code using frameworks such as React, Node.js, and Express.js.

- Work with databases like MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and MySQL to design and manage data structures.

- Participate in code reviews and provide constructive feedback.

- Fix bugs and optimize application performance.

## Required Skills and Qualifications

- Basic understanding of web development concepts.

- Familiarity with front-end technologies like HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (React, Vue.js, or Angular).

- Experience with back-end technologies like Node.js and Express.js.

- Familiarity with version control systems such as Git and GitHub.

- Good communication skills and the ability to work in a team.

- Willingness to learn and adapt to new technologies.

- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.

Apply
If you are passionate about web development and want to gain hands-on experience, we would love to hear from you. Please submit your resume and portfolio to careers@zeazh.com with the subject line "Full Stack Developer Intern Application".
