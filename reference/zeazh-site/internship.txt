# Frequently Asked Questions

All your questions answered. Still confused? Reach out to us on WhatsApp or via our website.

#### What is included in this internship + course program?

You'll go through structured training: 30 days of live and recorded classes with assignments and interactive sessions, followed by 30+ days of real-time project sprints with daily tasks and meetings.

#### What technologies will I learn?

You'll master GitHub, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Python, FastAPI, SQL, MongoDB (via ORMs), Docker, and Kubernetes — preparing you to build full-stack apps confidently.

#### What do I receive after completion?

You'll get a Course Completion Certificate, Internship Certificate, and a Letter of Recommendation. We also help with resume building and LinkedIn profile optimization.

#### How is the training conducted?

We use a blended model: Live classes for 30 days (also recorded), followed by daily sprint-based project training. This structure helps you apply what you learn, just like in real jobs.

#### Is this a paid program?

Yes, the total program fee is just ₹2000 — which includes the full course, internship training, certificates, mentorship, and career support.

#### Will I get placement support?

Yes, we provide career guidance through resume prep, LinkedIn profile building, mock interviews, and referrals for opportunities through our growing partner network.

#### Do I need prior experience to join?

No prior experience is required. Whether you're from a CS background or not, we train you from basics to advanced with full support at every step.

#### How do I enroll?

You can enroll directly via our website at https://zeazh.com/internship/PaymentForm or contact us through WhatsApp for help.
